# TerraWise real-data audit

Review date: 2026-11-11. The five-page original requirements PDF and all four reference images were reviewed. The reference images' soil, climate, yield and profit figures are illustrative and are not evidence or application data.

## Sources and data classes

| Source | Values and status | Verification and limitations |
|---|---|---|
| Farmer | Field name, point coordinates, area, irrigation category, crop history, priorities and optional soil entries. Soil provenance may be farmer-marked measured, estimated or unknown. | Input ranges, dates, units and required coordinates/water are checked. The app cannot verify a farmer's claim, lab quality, method compatibility or the field boundary. |
| NASA POWER monthly point API | Real historical gridded estimates when the public request succeeds: T2M, PRECTOTCORR, ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN and RH2M. Not direct on-farm measurements or a forecast. | NASA request parameters, conversions and completeness are tested with synthetic fixtures; an earlier live browser request for a test point and 2025 succeeded. Current verification results are in IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md. NASA may provide no data or rate-limit a request. |
| Exact NASA cache | Previously validated NASA response for the same request coordinates and historical years, retained in browser session storage; original retrieval date and cached status remain visible. | Parameter set is tied to the versioned cache key. Failures without a matching cache return an error; no artificial climate is supplied. Cache is not a durable backup. |
| Crop catalog and rules | Eight hardcoded *knowledge-base entries* and configurable, deterministic scoring rules. The output is calculated, provisional screening, not a measurement, forecast, probability, or trained AI. | Botanical identity and general rotation concepts have references. Individual numeric pH, N/P/K, water and temperature thresholds, windows, weights and repeat-family caps are **not sourced or validated for operational use**. Local agronomist review and rule-level citations remain required. |
| OpenStreetMap | Base-map tiles and attribution; a selected point is user-provided. | Tile service receives the map viewport. Map tiles can fail offline and do not verify parcel ownership or area. |
| Browser / optional Supabase | Guest field and analysis records, preferences and plans are stored locally; selected soil files use local IndexedDB. Supabase code and an owner-RLS SQL migration support account workspaces when configured. | There is no configured cloud service by default. Real auth, email, two-account isolation and deployment of RLS remain unverified without the operator's project. Soil files are not synced. |

## Suitability and uncertainty

Exploratory profiles require a complete 12-month NASA climate result or validated matching cache. Missing pH, N, P, K and recognized crop history remain unknown and unscored. The eight-category completeness indicator counts climate, pH, N, P, K, organic matter, recognized previous crop and water balance. It is separate from qualitative confidence and is neither statistical confidence nor scientific validation. A qualitative irrigation category and total rainfall cannot determine water compatibility. Water fit and overall suitability remain unknown and no ranked recommendations are generated until reviewed rules and a validated water balance are available. No yield, water savings, profit or fertilizer dosage is predicted.

[FAO on crop diversification](https://www.fao.org/conservation-agriculture/in-practice/species-diversification/en/) supports considering diverse crops and management objectives; [FAO's crop water needs](https://www.fao.org/4/S2022E/s2022e07.htm) describes indicative whole-growing-period ranges and cautions that duration matters. [University of Minnesota Extension on soil-test reports](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/crop-production/nutrient-management-for-minnesota-crops/understanding-the-soil-test-report) explains that test indexes and extraction methods vary. These sources do not validate this application's numerical rules. [NASA POWER monthly API documentation](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/monthly/) describes the data service.

## Legacy, production and outstanding setup

- New workspaces have no seeded fields or simulated climate. Untouched original starter fields are hidden by exact-record fingerprints. Edited fields derived from those starter IDs remain available for review, but are excluded from NASA requests, scores and totals until a user explicitly checks and corrects their location, soil, water and history. Old simulated and unreviewed analysis snapshots are hidden from the real-data interface and export. Existing stored legacy records are not automatically deleted; local records may still exist in browser storage. Synthetic fixtures reside under `src/test` or browser QA scripts only.
- The service worker cache was versioned for this transition. Existing offline installations need to reconnect and load the updated application before their old cached shell can be replaced; actual device update behavior still needs testing.
- Set the Supabase project URL and public publishable/anon key in `.env.local`, apply `supabase/migrations/20261111_workspace.sql`, configure auth redirects and email delivery, and verify anonymous and two-account access against the actual project. Never supply a service-role/secret key to Vite.
- The catalog needs qualified local review and per-rule evidence; water allocation, crop duration/overlap, pest-specific break periods, salinity, soil cover and nutrient carryover are not validated quantitative models. Without real lab results and crop history, suggestions are incomplete; without climate they are not generated.
- Arabic/RTL, real-device and accessibility audits, private cloud report storage, account deletion, security/backup operations and legal review remain open. Browser print/Save as PDF is available; dedicated PDF generation is not. No public deployment has been made.
